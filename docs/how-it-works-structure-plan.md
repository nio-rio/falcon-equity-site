## Deliberately not in this plan

- No testimonials, client stories or quotes (`CLIENT_STORIES` and `TESTIMONIALS` stay off this page). Nothing about real applicants.
- No 98% approval figure, no consolidation savings figure, no satisfaction percentage.
- No rate, factor rate or APR anywhere.
- No "same day" as a sitewide promise — that conflict is Ling's to settle.
- No team section, no founder credit (that belongs on About), no FAQ repeating product-specific terms.
