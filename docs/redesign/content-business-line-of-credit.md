## What it helps
- Label: What it helps
- Heading: When a line of credit is the right fit.
- Intro: Four moments when you need money available, not money delivered all at once.
- Making payroll before invoices land
	- Draw what you need to cover payroll, repay it when the receivables come in, and the money is available again for next time.
- Restocking inventory this week
	- A revolving line lets you draw for stock and pay for only the part you use, then draw again for the next order.
- Getting through an uneven year
	- Some months carry the year and some do not. Keep the line on standby and draw during the quiet stretches.
- Keeping a cushion for the unexpected
	- A repair, a deposit or a supplier who needs paying early — the line is already open when something lands.

## When a line of credit isn't the right fit.
- Label: When a line of credit isn't the right fit.
- Heading: A line is built for money you use and repay in pieces. Here are three situations where another Falcon product fits better.
- If you need one lump sum for a planned purchase
	- A term loan delivers the whole amount with a set repayment schedule, which suits an expansion or a one-time investment.
	- Link: Business Term Loan → /services/business-term-loan
- If what you are buying is the asset itself
	- Equipment, vehicles and commercial property can secure their own financing, so the cost is tied to the thing you are buying.
	- Link: Collateral & Asset Loans → /services/collateral-asset-loans
- If you are carrying more than one advance
	- Several daily payments stacking on top of each other is how a good month turns into a bad one. Consolidation rolls them into one payment.
	- Link: Consolidation of Positions → /services/consolidation-of-positions

## FAQ (expandable)
- Label: FAQ · Heading: Line of credit questions
- How is a line of credit different from a loan? A loan delivers a lump sum up front. A line of credit lets you draw funds as needed up to your limit, and you pay interest only on what you draw.
- Can I draw again after I repay? Yes. The line is revolving, so repaid amounts become available to draw again, subject to your agreement.
- What do I need to qualify? Typically 6+ months in business, $10K+ in monthly revenue and an active bank account. Final approval depends on underwriting.
- How fast can I get funded? Funding can happen the same day once you are approved and have signed. Timing depends on how quickly you send your documents.
- What does it cost? Falcon charges a 0% agreement fee. Rates are competitive, and no specific rate is published on this page — you pay for what you draw, and your specialist shows you the cost before you sign.
- Is the line secured or unsecured? Both options exist. An unsecured line does not put business assets behind it; a secured line does. Your specialist will explain which fits your file.
