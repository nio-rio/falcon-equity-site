## What it helps
- Label: What it helps
- Heading: When a term loan is the right fit.
- Intro: Four moments when you know the number, you know the timeline, and you would rather not carry a daily payment.
- Opening a new location
	- A term loan delivers the whole amount up front, so a lease, a fit-out and the first months of rent can be planned together.
- Remodeling or upgrading a facility
	- A set schedule over 6 to 36 months suits a build that should be paid for over time, not in a season.
- Hiring ahead of the work
	- Structured capital can cover new payroll while the revenue those people bring in catches up.
- Making a large, planned purchase
	- When the cost is known and the timing is yours, a fixed schedule is easier to run a business against.

## When a term loan isn't the right fit.
- Label: When a term loan isn't the right fit.
- Heading: A term loan hands you the money once. Here are three situations where another Falcon product fits better.
- If you need to draw, repay and draw again
	- A line of credit is revolving: draw what you need, pay for what you use, and the rest stays available.
	- Link: Business Line of Credit → /services/business-line-of-credit
- If what you are buying is the asset itself
	- Equipment, vehicles and commercial property can secure their own financing, so the cost is tied to the thing you are buying.
	- Link: Collateral & Asset Loans → /services/collateral-asset-loans
- If the money has to arrive today
	- A short-term loan is one option inside this product, and a merchant cash advance is built for funding the same day once you are approved and signed.
	- Link: Merchant Cash Advance → /services/merchant-cash-advance

## FAQ (expandable)
- Label: FAQ · Heading: Term loan questions
- What is the difference between a term loan and a short-term loan? Term loans typically run $10,000 – $2,000,000 with 6 – 36 month terms. Funding is often about 2 days after approval; some files fund the same day. Final terms depend on underwriting.
- What rate will I get? Rates are competitive. They depend on the product, term and your business profile, and no specific rate is published on this page. Your specialist will show you the full cost of any offer before you sign.
- What credit score do I need? Typically 550 or higher, along with 6+ months in business and $15K+ monthly revenue. Final terms depend on underwriting.
- Can I pay it off early? Prepayment terms vary by agreement. Your specialist will explain them before you sign.
- What does it cost? Falcon charges a 0% agreement fee. The cost of the loan itself is disclosed in full before you sign.
- What do I need to apply? Six months of business bank statements, a government-issued photo ID, a voided check and a list of any open advances or loans.
