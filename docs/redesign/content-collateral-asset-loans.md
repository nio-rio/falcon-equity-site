## What it helps
- Label: What it helps
- Heading: When a collateral or asset loan is the right fit.
- Intro: Four moments when what you already own is the fastest route to capital.
- Buying or upgrading equipment and vehicles
	- The thing you are buying becomes the security behind the loan, so a machine, a vehicle or a fit-out can be paid for out of what it will earn.
- Opening a second location
	- A collateral-secured loan can put commercial real estate to work for the expansion, instead of holding cash back for years.
- Unlocking equity in commercial real estate
	- If you own the building, the equity in it can fund growth somewhere else without selling the property.
- Financing technology and infrastructure
	- Servers, software and infrastructure earn for years. Spreading the cost over that time keeps working cash inside the business.

## When a collateral or asset loan isn't the right fit.
- Label: When a collateral or asset loan isn't the right fit.
- Heading: This product is tied to something you own. That is the point, and it is also the limit. Here are three situations where another Falcon product fits better.
- If you have nothing you want to pledge
	- A line of credit comes in an unsecured option, so you are not putting equipment or property behind it.
	- Link: Business Line of Credit → /services/business-line-of-credit
- If the cost is small and comes round often
	- Small recurring costs are cheaper to draw and repay as you go than to tie to an asset. A line of credit is built for that rhythm.
	- Link: Business Line of Credit → /services/business-line-of-credit
- If the money you are owed is the real problem
	- When the cash exists but sits in unpaid invoices, factoring sells those invoices instead of borrowing against equipment.
	- Link: Invoice Factoring Tool → /services/invoice-factoring-tool

## FAQ (expandable)
- Label: FAQ · Heading: Collateral and asset loan questions
- What can I use as collateral? Commercial real estate, equipment, vehicles and other business assets may qualify. Your specialist will confirm what fits your file.
- Do I need an equipment quote to apply? For equipment purchases, yes. Equipment quote or invoice helps underwriting size the loan correctly.
- How long are the terms? Terms run 12 – 60 months. Final terms depend on the asset and your business profile.
- How fast is funding? Equipment financing is typically funded in about 2 days. Real estate loans take about 2 weeks.
- What does it cost? Falcon charges a 0% agreement fee. Rates are competitive, and no specific rate is published on this page — your specialist shows you the full cost of the loan before you sign.
- What do I need to apply? Equipment quote or invoice, six months of business bank statements, a government-issued photo ID, a voided check and a list of any open advances or loans.
