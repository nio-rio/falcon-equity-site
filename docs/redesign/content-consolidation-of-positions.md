## What it helps
- Label: What it helps
- Heading: When consolidation is the right fit.
- Intro: Four moments when the problem is not the amount you owe, it is how many times a week it leaves the account.
- Replacing several daily payments with one
	- Each advance takes its own bite. Consolidation pays those positions off and rolls them into a single payment.
- Lowering what leaves the account each day or week
	- A single position can be sized against your sales, so the total coming out is easier to live with than the stack. Falcon clients save 30%–50% on average on their daily or weekly payments.
- Freeing up cash flow for operations
	- Money that was servicing four payments can go back into stock, payroll and the work that earns.
- Getting off the stacking treadmill
	- Taking a new advance to cover an old one is how the stack grows. One position ends that cycle.

## When consolidation isn't the right fit.
- Label: When consolidation isn't the right fit.
- Heading: Consolidation exists to fix a stack. If there is no stack, there is usually nothing to fix. Here are three situations where another Falcon product fits better.
- If you only have one position
	- Restructuring a single advance into a new one mostly adds cost. A line of credit or a term loan is a cleaner way to change how you pay.
	- Link: Business Line of Credit → /services/business-line-of-credit
- If the payment is already comfortable and you need new money
	- When the current schedule is not hurting, the real need is capital. A term loan delivers it with its own schedule.
	- Link: Business Term Loan → /services/business-term-loan
- If the costs are planned and repeating
	- Rent, payroll and regular inventory come round on a schedule. A line of credit lets you draw for each one and pay for what you use.
	- Link: Business Line of Credit → /services/business-line-of-credit

## FAQ (expandable)
- Label: FAQ · Heading: Consolidation questions
- Do I need to stop taking new advances? Consolidation works best when it replaces stacking. Your specialist will walk through a plan that keeps cash flow healthy.
- What information do I need? Recent bank statements and the details of each open position, such as the funder, balance and payment amount.
- What happens to my existing positions? They are paid off or restructured as part of the new position, so you are left with one payment instead of several.
- How much will I save? Falcon clients save 30%–50% on average on their current daily or weekly payments. Your actual savings depend on your existing positions and underwriting, so your specialist models the new payment against your current ones and shows you both before you sign.
- What does it cost? Falcon charges a 0% agreement fee. Rates are competitive, and no specific rate is published on this page. The full cost of the new position is disclosed before you sign.
- What do I need to apply? Six months of business bank statements, a government-issued photo ID, a voided check and the funder, balance and payment amount for every open position.
