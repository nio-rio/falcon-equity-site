# Retail industry page structure plan — `/industries/retail`

Copy contract: the nio rio Notion page **“Retail industry page – content outline (stage 1)”** (`401eacd0-19c7-8138-92d7-f3a1ecc82710`). This plan does not rewrite copy; it maps each section to a measured Blackstone layout and records where Falcon's content forces an adaptation.

Hard rules: figures from `src/data/services.ts` (word for word) and `src/data/site.ts` `STATS` only. Fees always **0% agreement fee**; rates "competitive"; no specific rate. No invented people, quotes, client names or results. No disclaimer or asterisk beside a CTA — `PAGE_DISCLOSURE` in the footer only. Placeholder images only, never images of people. Tokens and components from the design system; the home page stays frozen.

Route note: `/industries/*` currently redirects to `/#industries-heading` in **three** places — the retired-industries loop and `livePaths` in `astro.config.mjs`, `netlify.toml` (`/industries` and `/industries/*`), and `public/_redirects`. The build must lift the retail slug out of all three in the same commit, exactly as each service page did, or the route builds as a 299-byte meta-refresh stub.

**Breadcrumb decision:** `/industries` has no page and nothing is planned for it in this pass, so the breadcrumb renders **Home / Industries / Retail business loans** with *Industries* as plain text, not a link. A BreadcrumbList node still describes the hierarchy. No dead link ships.

Order: Hero → Answer first → Four cash pressures → The retail year → Which product fits your situation → The three products → When another product fits better → Three retail files → What you need → FAQ → SiteCta.

## Open items for Ling (not blocking the build)

1. **Section 4 kept.** The outline offers cutting it for length. It is kept because it is the page's strongest recognition device; if she wants a shorter page, section 4 is a single self-contained block and comes out without touching the others.
2. **Retail premises row.** Collateral & Asset Loans covers real-estate-secured lending per `CLAUDE.md`, but the published requirement line is equipment-only. The situation table therefore says nothing about borrowing against a building. One spec line from Ling turns that into a converting row.
3. **Card processor.** The advance rows imply a card-heavy store. If Falcon requires a particular processor relationship, two rows need rewording.
4. **Industries index.** `/industries` stays a redirect. When it ships, the breadcrumb's middle item becomes a link.
