Frequently Asked Questions

What is an evergreen fund?

An evergreen fund is a private market investment vehicle with an open‑ended structure that allows investors to subscribe or redeem capital periodically, rather than committing capital for a fixed term. Unlike traditional private funds, evergreen funds are designed to maintain a continuously invested portfolio, with capital entering and exiting the fund over time, making them more accessible for investors seeking long‑term exposure with some level of ongoing liquidity, subject to limits at the manager’s discretion.

What is a drawdown fund?

A drawdown fund is a closed‑end private market fund in which investors commit capital upfront that is called, or “drawn down,” over time as investment opportunities are identified. These funds typically have a defined investment period and lifespan, followed by a harvesting and distribution phase, and are structured for long‑term investors who can commit capital for several years with limited liquidity.
