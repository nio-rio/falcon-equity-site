TDI

Transmission Developers (TDI) is a developer of clean energy transmission projects including the Champlain Hudson Power Express (CHPE), an underground transmission line that will bring hydropower from Quebec to New York City. CHPE is expected to create enough energy to power over one million homes, supporting New York State’s clean energy transition.

Ancestry

Ancestry provides digital family history services to more than 3.6 million subscribers in over 30 countries. Ancestry’s large user network, unique content and scaled technology platform have turned it into an industry leader. We look forward to investing in new product development and resources to help Ancestry’s customers learn more about their family histories and themselves.
