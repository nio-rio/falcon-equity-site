The fastest funding is rarely about luck. It is about having your file **ready before you apply**. When underwriters get everything they need on day one, there is no back-and-forth, and qualified businesses can be funded in as little as **24–48 hours**.

Use this checklist to get ready.

## 1. Business basics

- [ ] **Legal business name** and any DBA
- [ ] **Business address** and phone number
- [ ] **EIN** (Employer Identification Number)
- [ ] **Business start date**, so underwriters can confirm time in business
- [ ] **Industry** and a one-line description of what you do

## 2. Owner information

- [ ] **Full name, home address and date of birth** for each owner with 20%+ ownership
- [ ] **Ownership percentage** for each owner
- [ ] A valid **government-issued photo ID**
- [ ] A **voided check** from the business bank account
- [ ] A **debt schedule** of any open advances or loans (lender, amount, date, remaining balance)
- [ ] **Ownership percentage** for each owner, plus co-borrower details if anyone else is on the file
- [ ] Answers on **bankruptcy and tax liens**, if applicable

## 3. Bank statements

This is the single most important item for most products.

- [ ] Your **last 6 months** of business bank statements (complete PDFs, every page)
- [ ] The **current month-to-date** activity, if requested
- [ ] Statements for **every business account** that receives deposits

**Tip:** download statements directly from your bank as PDFs. Screenshots and edited files slow things down.

## 4. Details about any open positions

If you already have advances or loans:

- [ ] The **funder's name** for each position
- [ ] The **current balance**
- [ ] The **payment amount and frequency** (daily, weekly or monthly)

Have several open positions? Ask about [Consolidation of Positions](/services/consolidation-of-positions). Falcon clients save 30%–50% on average on daily or weekly payments.

## 5. Product-specific documents

Depending on what you apply for, you may also need:

- **Collateral & Asset Loans:** an equipment quote or invoice, or details about the asset
- **Invoice Factoring:** an accounts receivable aging report and copies of the invoices
- **Business Term Loan:** recent business tax returns may be requested for larger amounts

## 6. Know your numbers

Underwriters will ask, so have these ready:

- [ ] **Average monthly revenue** over the last few months
- [ ] **How much you need** and **what it's for**
- [ ] A rough idea of **the payment you can comfortably support**

Not sure what payment fits? Try our [loan calculator](/resources/loan-calculator).

## 7. Be reachable

Speed depends on quick answers. After you apply:

- [ ] Keep your **phone nearby** and check your **email**, including spam
- [ ] Respond to document requests **the same day**
- [ ] Review and **sign your agreement** promptly once you approve an offer

## Common things that slow funding down

- Missing pages in bank statements
- Mismatched business names across documents
- Undisclosed open positions
- Waiting days to respond to a request

Avoid these and you give yourself the best chance at a fast decision.
